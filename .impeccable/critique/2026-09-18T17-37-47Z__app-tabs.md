## Priority Issues

**[P0] SetRow content overflows and clips at phone width.** Reproduced three separate ways: Assessment A saw it visually on Train; Assessment B independently reproduced the same overflow family on Progress ("Performance, n[oise]", stat tiles cut off) and Coach (quick-reply chip row, ~2.5 of 7 chips visible, no scroll cue) at 375x812. Why it matters: this is the app's own highest-priority screen per PRODUCT.md, shipping broken. Fix: constrain SetRow's row children (setLabel 52px + two flex fields + 40 + 28 + gaps) and audit the Progress trend-bar row and Coach's chip row for the same unconstrained-width cause. Suggested command: /impeccable harden.

**[P1] Complete/remove controls collide, both under the project's own touch-target floor.** SetRow.tsx:112-114 (check, 40x40) and :126-128 (remove, 28x28) sit ~8px apart — both below the 44pt guideline and the project's own touchTarget.minHeight: 48 token (tokens.ts:117-119). Why it matters: a mis-tap during a real workout (sweaty hands, rest-timer urgency) silently deletes a set with no undo. Fix: enlarge both to >=44pt, increase separation, add an undo toast or require long-press to delete. Suggested command: /impeccable harden.

**[P1] Home's hero title overwhelms the fold.** At 30px/800-weight, "WORKOUT 15 / Strength + Conditioning — Push / Pull / Carry" wraps to 4 lines, pushing Start Workout below the fold pre-scroll on a 375x812 viewport. Fix: reserve the large size for "WORKOUT N" alone, drop the focus line to headline scale. Suggested command: /impeccable layout.

**[P2] Coach's 7 quick-action chips violate the <=4 working-memory rule** and fire immediately with no confirm step, inconsistent with the Apply-gate pattern used everywhere else in Coach. Fix: cap visible chips to 4 + "More," or route them through the same explicit-confirm pattern. Suggested command: /impeccable clarify.

**[P3] Set completion has no haptic or motion payoff** despite DESIGN.md naming haptic feedback as a north-star trait — the check flips instantly. Fix: add the documented light haptic + a brief motion.fast (140ms) fill transition. Suggested command: /impeccable delight.
